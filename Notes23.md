# 2. 📅 2026 IMPORTANT PYQ PATTERNS

## TCS – 2026 Pattern

### Q1. Percentage

A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net change?

**Answer:** 4% decrease

### Solution

Assume number = 100.

After 20% increase:

100 + 20 = 120

After 20% decrease:

120 - 24 = 96

Net decrease = 4.

**Final Answer = 4% decrease**

---

### Q2. Time & Work

A can complete a work in 10 days and B can complete it in 15 days. How many days will they take together?

A's work/day = 1/10

B's work/day = 1/15

Together:

1/10 + 1/15

= 3/30 + 2/30

= 5/30

= 1/6

**Answer: 6 days**

---

### Q3. Number System

Find the remainder when 2^10 is divided by 7.

2^3 = 8 ≡ 1 (mod 7)

Therefore:

2^9 ≡ 1

2^10 ≡ 2

**Answer: 2**

---

### Q4. Average

The average of five numbers is 24. If one number is removed, the average becomes 20. Find the removed number.

Total = 5 × 24 = 120

Remaining total = 4 × 20 = 80

Removed number:

120 - 80 = 40

**Answer: 40**

---

## Infosys – 2026 Pattern

### Q5. Profit & Loss

An article is bought for ₹800 and sold for ₹920. Find the profit percentage.

Profit = 920 - 800 = ₹120

Profit %:

120/800 × 100

= 15%

**Answer: 15%**

---

### Q6. Ratio

The ratio of boys to girls is 3:2. If there are 40 students, how many are boys?

Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5

Boys:

3/5 × 40 = 24

**Answer: 24**

---

### Q7. Probability

A die is thrown once. What is the probability of getting an even number?

Even numbers = 2, 4, 6

Favourable outcomes = 3

Total outcomes = 6

Probability = 3/6 = 1/2

**Answer: 1/2**

# 19. 🧮 MUST-MEMORIZE FORMULAS

## Percentage

Percentage = (Part / Total) × 100

---

## Profit

Profit = SP - CP

Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100

---

## Loss

Loss = CP - SP

Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100

---

## Discount

Discount = MP - SP

Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100

---

## Simple Interest

SI = P × R × T / 100

---

## Compound Interest

Amount = P(1 + R/100)^T

CI = Amount - Principal

---

## Average

Average = Sum / Number of observations

---

## Speed

Speed = Distance / Time

---

## Distance

Distance = Speed × Time

---

## Time

Time = Distance / Speed

---

## Work

If A completes work in x days:

A's one-day work = 1/x

---

## Probability

Probability = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes

---

## Permutation

nPr = n!/(n-r)!

---

## Combination

nCr = n!/[r!(n-r)!]
